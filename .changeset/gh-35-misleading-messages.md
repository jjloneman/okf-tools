Messages that misled are corrected: a status OKF doesn't define is named in the hint instead of "no status", a record new to the repo is no longer warned about as having other uncommitted changes, `--bundle ""` is a usage error, the "No bundles" and "No one to sign as" errors name the `package.json` they read, `--help` beside a bad flag prints the help, and a timestamp ending in a no-break space is renewed instead of refused.
